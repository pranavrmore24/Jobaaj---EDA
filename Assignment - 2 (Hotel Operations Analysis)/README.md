# Hotel Operation Analysis - EDA Project

## Overview

This project performs an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a Hotel Booking Demand dataset to understand customer booking behavior, cancellation patterns, revenue trends, market segment performance, and hotel operations. The analysis includes data cleaning, visualization, statistical analysis, and machine learning techniques to generate actionable business insights that help hotel management optimize pricing, improve customer retention, reduce cancellations, and enhance revenue management strategies.

## Objective

- Analyze hotel booking patterns and customer behavior.
- Identify factors influencing booking cancellations.
- Examine revenue trends using Average Daily Rate (ADR).
- Understand customer preferences across market segments and room types.
- Evaluate seasonal trends in bookings and cancellations.
- Generate data-driven recommendations for improving hotel operations and profitability.

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Scikit-Learn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Process

1. Data Cleaning
   - Removed duplicate records.
   - Handled missing values.
   - Removed invalid ADR values.
   - Created new features such as Total Stay Duration.
   - Standardized dataset fields.

2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
   - Analyzed booking patterns and customer behavior.
   - Examined cancellation trends.
   - Evaluated ADR across different hotel and customer segments.
   - Studied room type and market segment performance.

3. Intermediate Analysis
   - Lead Time vs Cancellation Analysis.
   - ADR by Market Segment.
   - Revenue Trend Analysis.
   - Stay Duration Analysis.
   - Distribution Channel Analysis.

4. Advanced Analysis
   - Logistic Regression for Cancellation Prediction.
   - Linear Regression for ADR Analysis.
   - Seasonal Cancellation Analysis.
   - Market Segment Lead Time Analysis.
   - Guest Satisfaction Analysis.

5. Business Insights & Recommendations
   - Generated actionable recommendations based on analytical findings.

## Key Insights

- City Hotels receive more bookings and have higher ADR than Resort Hotels.
- Portugal generated the highest number of hotel bookings.
- August recorded the highest booking volume and revenue.
- Room Type A accounted for approximately 65% of all bookings.
- Previous cancellations are the strongest predictor of future cancellations.
- Long lead-time bookings are more likely to be canceled.
- Guests with more special requests tend to generate higher ADR.
- Family bookings, especially those with children, contribute significantly to higher room revenue.
- TA/TO is the dominant booking distribution channel.
- Group customers book much earlier than other market segments.
- Cancellation rates peak during July and August.
- New guests stay longer on average than repeated guests.

## Project Outcome

This project successfully identified key factors affecting hotel bookings, cancellations, pricing, and customer behavior. The insights can help hotel management improve revenue optimization, customer satisfaction, booking retention, and operational efficiency through data-driven decision-making.
